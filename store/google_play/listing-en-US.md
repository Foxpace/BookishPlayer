## Full description

Bookish is an offline-first player for the audiobooks you already own. Import audio files from your device or cloud file provider, then listen without depending on a subscription or a permanent internet connection.

Keep your library organized

• Import MP3, M4A, M4B, AAC, FLAC, WAV, OGG, and Opus files
• Use single-file books or multi-file collections
• Browse in a grid or list, search your library, and group books by author, series, folder, or listening status
• Edit titles, authors, series, folders, covers, track order, and chapters

Listen your way

• Navigate by chapter or position
• Adjust playback speed from 0.75x to 2x
• Configure short and large seek jumps
• Use fixed or end-of-chapter sleep timers with a gentle fade-out
• Continue automatically with the next book in a series
• Control playback from your headset, lock screen, notification, Android Auto, or CarPlay
• On Android, optionally shorten silence and boost voices

Remember the useful parts

• Add timestamped text or voice notes while listening
• Return to the exact position behind any note
• Edit and share individual notes
• Export a book's notes as Markdown

See your listening history

Review weekly, monthly, and yearly activity, total listening time, active days, and completed books.

Your library stays under your control

Bookish copies imported audio to app-private storage for reliable offline playback. Your library, progress, notes, history, and preferences stay on your device. Export and restore a JSON backup when you want to move or safeguard that data. Storage tools help you find missing books, duplicates, and unused files.

Choose light, dark, or system appearance. Bookish follows your device language and supports English and Slovak.

Audiobooks are not included. Import audio files you own or are permitted to use.
