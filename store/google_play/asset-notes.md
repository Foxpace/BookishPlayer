## Rights record

- App icon: first-party Bookish artwork from `apps/store/assets/icon/bookish_app_icon.png`.
- Interface screenshots: first-party Bookish interface generated from deterministic Flutter fixtures.
- Sample book: the screenshots use the public-domain LibriVox recording of _Alice's Adventures in Wonderland_ by Lewis Carroll, as documented in the repository README.
- Promotional layouts: generated locally by `prepare.sh` from the first-party icon and screenshots. No stock art, store badges, rankings, prices, testimonials, or third-party product logos are added.
