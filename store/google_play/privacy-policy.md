# Privacy policy for Bookish

Effective date: `[DATE]`

Bookish is published by `[PUBLISHER NAME]`. This policy explains how the Google Play version of Bookish handles information.

## Information Bookish handles

Bookish works with audiobook files that you choose, library metadata, cover images, playback position and history, text or voice notes, listening statistics, backups, and app preferences. Bookish stores this information locally on your device. The publisher does not receive it and does not operate a Bookish account or cloud service.

## Data collection and sharing

The publisher does not collect, sell, or share personal information through Bookish. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

When you deliberately export a backup or notes, or share a note, Android sends the selected content to the location or app you choose. The receiving app or service handles that content under its own privacy policy.

## Device permissions

Bookish may request access needed for these functions:

- Microphone access records a voice note after you choose to start recording.
- File access uses Android's system picker so you can choose audiobook, cover, or backup files and export data.
- Notification and foreground-service access keeps audiobook playback available and controllable in the background.
- Nearby-device or Bluetooth access supports audio-output selection and media controls where Android requires it.
- Wake-lock access keeps active playback running while the screen is off.

Bookish does not use these permissions to send your library or listening data to the publisher.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete individual books and notes, choose whether imported audio files are deleted, clean unused files, or reset all Bookish data. Uninstalling the app also removes app-private data according to Android's normal behavior. Files you exported outside Bookish must be deleted from their destination separately.

## Children's privacy

Bookish is not directed to children under 13. The publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to this policy

If Bookish's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before the affected release is published.
